# Building Websites: Normal vs. Web Apps

This video explains the key differences between building normal websites and web applications (web apps), outlining the distinct design considerations, technical skills, and development processes involved in each.

## I. Website Types and Objectives [00:00:00]

* **Normal Websites:** Primarily for marketing, branding, and information sharing.  Think digital magazines or banners – design-focused and less interactive.  Examples include landing pages (like Apple's) and informational sites.
* **Web Apps:** Function as tools or products, offering high interactivity and dynamic content. Examples include online stores, social media platforms (like YouTube), and games.  They provide tailored experiences based on user data.
* **Hybrids:** Some websites blend both approaches, such as photo and music libraries, combining media with interactive elements.

## II. Building Process: Key Stages [00:00:30]

### A. Defining Objectives [00:00:45]

* **Normal Websites:** Focus on target audience, competition, and keywords.
* **Web Apps:** Require extensive research into user experience (UX), tech stack, performance optimization, and market demand validation.

### B. Research and Design [00:01:15]

* **Normal Websites:** Design emphasizes visuals, graphics, fonts, and clear messaging.  The focus is on aesthetics and communicating information effectively.
* **Web Apps:** Prioritizes simplicity, functionality, speed, and mobile-friendliness.  Design is intuitive and supports user task completion.  Examples comparing Apple's landing page (design-heavy) and YouTube (simpler, functional).

### C. Design Systems [00:02:15]

The video emphasizes the importance of design systems for both website types.  This involves identifying and creating reusable components (buttons, sections, etc.) to ensure consistency and efficient development.  The process is illustrated using examples from Apple's website and YouTube.

### D. Implementation and Maintenance [00:03:30]

* **Normal Websites:** Typically require HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.  Simpler websites might be built in a few days or weeks.
* **Web Apps:** Can involve months or years of development and require ongoing maintenance and updates.  More complex web apps may utilize frameworks like React or Angular, and often need a larger team.  The video uses the example of Wikipedia (a normal website requiring a complex tech stack) and YouTube (a web app).  It also discusses the MERN stack for building web apps.

## III. Skill Sets and Technologies [00:04:30]

* **Normal Websites:** Require design skills (graphics, typography, marketing), alongside front-end development skills (HTML, CSS, JavaScript).
* **Web Apps:** Demand stronger analytical and problem-solving skills, focused on software development and implementing features.  This often involves using a variety of frameworks and technologies beyond basic front-end languages.

## IV. Conclusion [00:05:00]

The video concludes by highlighting the fundamental differences between building normal websites (design and content-focused) and web apps (functionality and interaction-focused).  It emphasizes that while some projects blend both, web app development often represents a more complex and lucrative career path in the tech industry.
