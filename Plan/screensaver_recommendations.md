# Recommended Screensaver Animations

This document outlines lightweight, math-driven, and highly CPU-optimized screensaver animation concepts designed with a dark aesthetic and low power consumption.

---

## 1. Deep Sea Bioluminescent Jellyfish & Plankton
* **Visual Style:** Pitch-black deep ocean abyss (`#000208`).
* **Animation Mechanics:** Translucent, glowing neon-blue and purple jellyfish pulsate gently up and down using sine-wave oscillation, trailing delicate bioluminescent tentacles while microscopic plankton drifts lazily in ocean currents.
* **Performance Profile:** Extremely relaxing and visually striking; lightweight math requirements.

## 2. Quantum Particle Constellation (Neural Network)
* **Visual Style:** Minimalist dark space with floating geometric nodes.
* **Animation Mechanics:** 3D-projected glowing nodes drift across the screen. Nodes that come within proximity of each other connect via faint, delicate laser threads, creating a breathing, living neural lattice.
* **Performance Profile:** Futuristic and high-tech; optimized by calculating proximity checks only for nearby spatial partitions.

## 3. Hypnotic Torus / Wireframe Black Hole (Interstellar Style)
* **Visual Style:** Cinematic cosmic gravity simulation in a pure black void.
* **Animation Mechanics:** An accretion disk of glowing stardust rotates slowly, bending smoothly around an invisible gravitational center point using 3D rotation matrices.
* **Performance Profile:** High visual impact with minimal asset loading.

## 4. Zen Rain / Cosmic Ripples on Dark Water
* **Visual Style:** Mirror-like dark obsidian water surface reflecting distant starlight.
* **Animation Mechanics:** Infrequent raindrops ripple across the water surface, creating soft concentric rings of light that expand and fade gracefully.
* **Performance Profile:** Ultra-low CPU usage because calculations only trigger upon raindrop events, remaining completely idle between drops.
