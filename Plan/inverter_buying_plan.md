# Inverter Buying Plan for Pune, India Residence

## 1. Load Calculation & Requirements
- **Appliances to run during outage:**
  - 2 LED Lights (~10W each) = 20W
  - 2 Ceiling Fans (~75W each) = 150W
  - Wi-Fi Router = 15W
  - 2 Computer Monitors (~30W each) = 60W
  - Laptop & Mobile Charging = 80W
  - **Total Running Load:** ~325 Watts
- **Outage Profile:** 6 to 7 hours, twice a week.
- **Grid Specifications:** 230V, 50Hz (Standard Indian Grid).
- **Inverter Sizing:** 900 VA to 1100 VA (Pure Sine Wave recommended for sensitive electronics like monitors and routers).
- **Battery Capacity Needed:** ~200 Ah (to support 325W for 6.5 hours safely considering efficiency and discharge limits).

---

## 2. Option A: The Traditional & Economical Route (Tubular Lead-Acid)
- **Inverter:** Luminous Zelio+ 1100 (Pure Sine Wave) or Microtek Super Seva 1100. Features a clear digital display for backup time remaining.
- **Battery:** Exide InvaMaster IMTT2000 (200 Ah Tall Tubular) or Luminous 200 Ah Tall Tubular Battery.
- **Estimated Cost:** ₹23,000 - ₹26,000 (including battery trolley and installation).
- **Pros:**
  - Lower initial purchase cost.
  - Highly reliable with easy local servicing and parts availability across Pune.
  - Lifespan: 3 to 5 years (with proper maintenance).
- **Cons:**
  - Requires periodic maintenance (top-up with distilled water every 3–4 months).
  - Takes up floor space (heavy battery trolley required).
  - Slower charging times compared to lithium.

---

## 3. Option B: The Modern & Maintenance-Free Route (Lithium-Ion / LiFePO4)
- **System:** Integrated Lithium-ion Home UPS / Smart Energy Storage (e.g., Luminous / Microtek / Livguard Lithium home systems).
- **Capacity:** 1.5 kWh to 2 kWh equivalent usable energy storage.
- **Pros:**
  - **Zero Maintenance:** No water topping, zero gas emission, fully sealed.
  - **Long Lifespan:** 8 to 10+ years (2000+ cycles).
  - **Fast Charging:** Recharges significantly faster (crucial for frequent/back-to-back outages).
  - **Compact & Lightweight:** Sleek form factor, can be placed neatly or wall-mounted.
- **Cons:**
  - Higher upfront investment cost.
  - Less modular for local makeshift repairs (relies on brand service centers).

---

## 4. Next Steps & Recommendation
- For a **set-it-and-forget-it premium user**, Option B (Lithium-Ion) is superior for long-term convenience and lifespan.
- For a **cost-effective, highly serviceable local standard**, Option C / Option A (Luminous Zelio 1100 + Exide 200Ah Tubular Battery) is the most robust and popular choice in Pune.
