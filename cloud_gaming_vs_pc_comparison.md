## 🎮 Decision Matrix

| Gamer Profile | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Hardcore** (3-4+ hrs/day, competitive, modding, VR) | **BUILD PC** | Breaks even in 1.5 yrs; full control, zero latency, upgrade path |
| **Enthusiast** (2-3 hrs/day, mixed genres) | **LEAN PC** | Near break-even; consider used/refurbished to tip scales |
| **Casual** (1-2 hrs/day, story games, weekends) | **CLOUD** | PC never pays off; zero maintenance, play anywhere |
| **Uncertain / Transient** (moving, changing schedules) | **CLOUD FIRST** | Zero CAPEX commitment; migrate to PC when usage stabilizes |

## 🔄 Hybrid Strategy (Best of Both)

| Workload | Platform |
|----------|----------|
| **Competitive FPS** (Val, CS2, Apex) | **Local PC** — lowest latency, no compression artifacts |
| **New AAA Releases** (day-1, 100GB+) | **Cloud** — no download wait, no storage pressure |
| **Modded/Strategy/Indie** | **Local PC** — mod support, long sessions |
| **Travel / Second Screen** | **Cloud** — phone/tablet/TV continuation |
